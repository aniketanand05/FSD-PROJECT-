Why S' Residences?

S' Residences – Providing a Safe Haven for Dayananda Sagar University Students Education is an essential aspect of life, and students are the future of the world. As such, ensuring that they have access to adequate accommodation that provides safety, comfort, and convenience is essential. S’ Residences is a student housing facility that is located in Harohalli, Bangalore, providing comfortable and secure accommodation for Dayananda Sagar University students.
