About School of Engineering

School of Engineering under Dayananda Sagar University provides Science & Technology based Education that is required to develop high caliber Engineers suitable for Industry and Scientific Organization.

The curriculum focuses on knowledge based course work integrated with skill development as a part of training. It equally helps in inculcating the scientific temper necessary for the Lifelong learning process.
