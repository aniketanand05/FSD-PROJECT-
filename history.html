Founding Pillars

The Dayananda Sagar Schools and Colleges of Science, Arts and Commerce were established in the early nineteen sixties and have grown into one of India’s prominent education hubs under the Mahatma Gandhi Vidya Peetha Trust in Bengaluru.

Over the years, the institutions have expanded their reach across diverse disciplines, providing quality education and a vibrant learning ecosystem for students from different parts of the country and abroad.

Late Shri R Dayananda Sagar

Late Shri R Dayananda Sagar was the visionary founder whose dedication to education laid the strong foundation for the Dayananda Sagar group of institutions.

Late Smt Chandramma Sagar

Late Smt Chandramma Sagar supported and strengthened this vision, contributing to the growth of the institutions and their commitment to academic excellence.

Growth of the Institutions

The institutions offer programs ranging from pre-university to postgraduate and doctoral levels in multiple domains.

  • Engineering and Technology
  • Dental Sciences and Health Sciences
  • Biological Sciences and Allied Health
  • Arts, Commerce and Basic Sciences
  • Pharmacy, Nursing and Management Studies
  • Information Technology and Emerging Areas

New courses and research-oriented programs continue to be added in response to technological progress and industry needs.

Academic and Campus Environment

Students benefit from well-equipped classrooms, laboratories, libraries and computing facilities, complemented by active research and collaborative projects.

The campus also provides strong support systems including sports infrastructure, hostels and cultural activities to ensure holistic development.

Dayananda Sagar University: A New Phase

Building on the legacy of Dayananda Sagar Institutions, Dayananda Sagar University represents a new phase as a State Private University committed to nurturing global citizens.

In tune with initiatives such as Make in India and the start-up ecosystem, the university focuses on fostering innovation, entrepreneurship and leadership among students.

The academic framework encourages competency across multiple domains, with emphasis on technology, management, networking, marketing, finance and appreciation of art and culture.
