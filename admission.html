Eligibility Criteria (Sample)

  • UG: Must have passed 12th standard or equivalent with minimum 50% in relevant subjects
  • PG: Bachelor’s degree in relevant discipline with minimum 50% (aggregate)
  • For B.Tech: Mandatory subjects in 12th - Physics, Mathematics & Chemistry/Computer Science
  • For Medical/Health Sciences: NEET or equivalent as required by program
  • For MBA: Bachelor's degree + valid entrance exam scores (CAT/MAT/XAT etc.)

Documents Required: 12th marksheet, ID proof, recent photographs, caste certificate (if applicable), entrance exam scorecard.
